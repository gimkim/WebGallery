- User request: Add a log viewer and filtering system to the Management area.
- Concepts/rules established: `Management > Logs` is restricted to the Admin role and aggregates share activity across all owners without exposing share tokens. Filtering is server-side by local From/To dates, exact event type, owner, Folder/Collection share type, partial IP address, and a combined target/details/user/collection/visitor search. Filters persist through newest-first pagination at 100 rows per page. Summary cards reflect the filtered result. Existing owner-scoped Activity pages and clear behavior remain unchanged.

SQLite indexes cover Unix time, EventType+Unix time, and ClientIp+Unix time so date/event/IP filtering does not require loading the complete log or relying on SQLite `DateTimeOffset` comparisons.
